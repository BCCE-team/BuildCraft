# Contributors

Thanks to everyone who has contributed to BuildCraft Community Edition and helped make this project possible!

## BCCE Team

- **CurativeTree** — The project's original developer. Without his work, BCCE as we know it would not exist. He created the initial mod foundation for Minecraft 1.19.2, which was later refined and expanded into something much greater.

- **ShipovskijKorp** — Current project owner and lead maintainer. Responsible for the vast majority of development since May 2026. Also known as the inventor of the legendary "burnout engine".

- **Memesis414** — Mr.InternetIssues himself. Responsible for most of ITCE and approximately half of APCE. Occasionally contributes bug reports as well.

- **Smail** — One of the newest members of the team. His work laid the foundation for the ongoing development of the Fabric family. He has also contributed several pull requests.

## Other Contributors

- **NightOvl** — Helped with development and bug hunting during the project's earliest days. His contributions, while relatively modest in scale, were genuinely valuable to the project.

- **Louazgre** — The person who made Create compatibility possible. He proposed the original idea and demonstrated its implementation, which was subsequently refined and became an important part of the project.

- **Pietruszka33** — An active external tester who discovered and reported numerous bugs through both GitHub and Discord. His extensive bug hunting helped identify many issues that might otherwise have gone unnoticed.

- **Dom.** — Modpack developer whose ideas and feedback inspired the creation of the datapack-based recipe system and configurable game balance. Also reported several issues with robot AI.

- **Doommius** — Reported a security issue and helped resolve an item duplication exploit. His assistance was valuable in making the project more secure.

- **Jimmy** — Just some guy from the BuildCraft Discord server. Participated in discussions, suggested ideas, and occasionally talked me out of some pretty stupid decisions.

## And Many Others...

Thanks to everyone who opened issues, participated in discussions on Discord, left comments, starred our repositories, or created modpacks featuring BCCE.

Many people have contributed to this project in ways both big and small, and unfortunately, I can't list everyone here. This list highlights those whose contributions have had a particularly significant impact on the project's development.

I sincerely hope this list will continue to grow in the future.
